> **Engineered for Pan-African Health Impact** | *People | Partnerships | Systems*

## 📌 Executive Overview

**Centre for Integrated Health Programs (CIHP)** is a leading indigenous non-governmental organization promoting equitable, evidence-based public health solutions across **20 Nigerian states**, with a strategic ambition to establish physical operational footholds across **5 Pan-African nations**.

CIHP works in partnership with local and international governments, donors, civil society organizations, and communities to design, implement, and scale sustainable health interventions that improve outcomes for women, children, adolescents, and families.

## 📜 History & Institutional Evolution

CIHP was established in **2010** as part of a global movement to transfer donor-supported health program management to local indigenous institutions.

```

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      │
                                      ▼
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  2010 – 2022: Multi-State Scaling & Strategic Growth                          |
|  - Expanded across 20 states in HIV/AIDS, TB, RMNCH, & Digital Health          |

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      │
                                      ▼
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

|  - Launched REACHOUT (EU/EDCTP3), IMPAACT4HIV (Unitaid), AEARI, & Gates grants |
|  - Advancing 3-year strategic growth into 5 Pan-African footholds             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Key Milestones:
1. **2006 – 2010: Operational Foundations (ICAP at Columbia University)**
   - Operated under ICAP at Columbia University in Nigeria, building foundational HIV/AIDS and clinical infrastructure funded by PEPFAR.
2. **2010: Indigenous Transition**
   - Autonomous transition into a registered Nigerian non-profit organization to drive local ownership and sustainable funding stewardship.
3. **2010 – 2022: Multi-State Scale & Diverse Grant Stewardship**
   - Expanded technical coverage to RMNCH (Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, and Child Health), Tuberculosis control, laboratory diagnostics, and electronic health record systems.
4. **2025 – 2026 & Beyond: Global Consortia & Pan-African Expansion**
   - Leading multi-country research and implementation consortia across Europe and Africa (including Italy, Belgium, Germany, Switzerland, Rwanda, and Kenya).

## 🛠️ Technical Capabilities & Service Domains

### 1. HIV/AIDS & Tuberculosis Care and Prevention
- Direct service delivery, prevention of mother-to-child transmission (PMTCT), pediatric care, and antiretroviral therapy (ART) expansion.
- Community-level case finding, index testing, and differentiated service delivery models.

### 2. Reproductive, Maternal, Newborn, Child & Adolescent Health (RMNCAH)
- Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH) intervention bundles (E-MOTIVE scaling).
- Strengthening Special Care Baby Units (SCBU) and essential newborn care across target facilities.
- Adolescent & Young People (AYP) youth empowerment hubs.

### 3. Health Systems Strengthening (HSS) & Workforce Development
- Clinical mentorship, health workforce orientation, capacity building, and institutional policy advocacy.
- Supply chain logistics, drug management, and facility infrastructure upgrades.

### 4. Digital Health, Health Informatics & Surveillance
- Real-time data collection systems, electronic medical records (EMR), disease surveillance, and epidemic response tracking.

### 5. Laboratory Systems & Diagnostics Network
- Diagnostic network optimization, viral load testing, TB molecular diagnostics (GeneXpert), and quality assurance frameworks.

## 🚀 Flagship Projects & Grants

* **REACHOUT Project (2025–2028)**
  * *Funding/Partners:* European Union / EDCTP3 Consortium (Italy, Belgium, Germany, Switzerland, Rwanda, Kenya, Nigeria)
  * *Focus:* Implementation research and health systems strengthening for vulnerable populations.
* **IMPAACT4HIV Project**
  * *Funding/Partners:* Unitaid
  * *Focus:* Advanced HIV Disease (AHD) and pediatric TB integration.
* **AEARI Project**
  * *Focus:* Scaling PPH E-MOTIVE clinical care bundles across Lagos State facilities.
* **Gates Foundation Awards (1st - 4th Awards)**
  * *Funding/Partners:* Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation
  * *Focus:* Essential newborn care, SCBU strengthening, and maternal mortality reduction.
* **IRIS Project (2022–2027)**
  * *Focus:* Comprehensive PEPFAR/CDC community and facility HIV intervention.
* **TB-LON3 Project (2020–2025)**
  * *Focus:* USAID-supported tuberculosis local organization network expansion.
* **G-ANC Project (2022–2025)**
  * *Focus:* Group Antenatal Care innovations for improved maternal health outcomes.
