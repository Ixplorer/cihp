Geographic Reach & Growth

An African Institution Built on Accountable Delivery

CIHP is a Nigerian-led public health organization with a strong implementation footprint across 19 states and strategic partnerships spanning East Africa, Southern Africa, America, and Europe. By connecting local expertise with regional and global collaboration, we deliver scalable, evidence-driven solutions that strengthen health systems and improve health outcomes.

CIHP is building on its Nigerian foundation as it grows into a Pan-African public health powerhouse. Our strategic ambition is to establish physical operational footholds in at least 5 African countries over the next three years.

OPERATIONAL COVERAGE: 20 NIGERIAN STATES

Nigeria Operational Core

Providing comprehensive health interventions, HIV/TB care, Maternal & Child Health, and health systems strengthening across 20 state jurisdictions.

Vision, Mission & Guiding Principles

The Foundation of Our Public Health Mandate

CIHP’s work is driven by unwavering values, rigorous evidence, and a deep commitment to transforming lives and building resilient healthcare systems.

Our Vision

Audaciously transforming lives through partnership and value creation for healthier communities and a thriving Africa

Our Mission

To transform lives, especially the most vulnerable, through cutting-edge solutions, people-centred and sustainable health systems, delivered through strategic partnerships.

The C-I-H-P Core Values Matrix

REACHOUT Project (EU / EDCTP3)

Flagship European Union-funded multi-country initiative tackling zero-dose immunization gaps across Nigeria, Kenya, and Rwanda.

  • Funder & Consortium: European Union (Global Health EDCTP3), Università Bocconi, AMREF, Swiss-TPH
  • Scope: Multi-country implementation research, precision data & behavioral insights

Active (2023 - 2026)

AEARI Project (Lagos RMNCH)

Accelerating Expanded Adoption of RMNCH Innovations (PPH E-MOTIVE Bundle, GANC & BEmONC) across 20 Lagos State LGAs.

  • Funder & Partners: Gates Foundation, Lagos State MoH, LSPHCB & TAConnect
  • Key Metrics: 400 workers trained, 37% decision competency gain, 67 PHC sites
  • Evidence Source: Lagos State RMNCH Innovation Evaluation
View Project Brief
Active (2024 - 2027)

IMPAACT4HIV (Pediatric AHD)

Deploying the WHO STOP AIDS Toolkit to diagnose and treat Advanced HIV Disease and TB in children & adolescents across 21 facilities.

  • Funder & Partners: Unitaid, FMoH&SW, NASCP, PATA & Aurum Institute
  • Key Metrics: 101 CLHIV identified, 100% treatment rate, 221 providers trained
  • Evidence Source: Unitaid Pediatric AHD Program Audit
