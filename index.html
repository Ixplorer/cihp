About CIHP - Centre for Integrated Health Programs

An African Institution Built
on Accountable Delivery

CIHP is a Nigerian-led public health organization with a strong implementation footprint across 20 states and strategic partnerships spanning East Africa, Southern Africa, America, and Europe. By connecting local expertise with regional and global collaboration, we deliver scalable, evidence-driven solutions that strengthen health systems and improve health outcomes.

CIHP is building on its Nigerian foundation as it grows into a Pan-African public health powerhouse. Our strategic ambition is to establish physical operational footholds in at least 5 African countries over the next three years.
