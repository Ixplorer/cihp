Registration Guidelines

Pre-Qualification Criteria & Requirements

CIHP invites competent and eligible insurance firms and brokerage entities to pre-qualify for vendor registration. Evaluators will strictly review provided evidence without assumptions.

Target Service Categories

Firms may apply for one or both of the following pre-qualification lots:

  • Item 1: Group Life Insurance (CIHP supported locations/states & Central Office, Abuja).
  • Item 2: Insurance Brokerage (Comprehensive Physical Assets).

Statutory Requirements

Mandatory legal documentation required for submission:

  • CAC Registration (Certificate of Incorporation, CAC2, CAC7, Art & Memo).
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN) with 3-year Tax Clearance Certificates.
  • VAT Registration and evidence of VAT remittance.
  • Regulatory Authority License (NAICOM / NCRIB license).

Financial & Operational Capacity

Proof of corporate stability and past performance track record:

  • Audited Financial Statements for the last three (3) years.
  • Evidence of active corporate bank account in company name.
  • Verifiable list of similar jobs executed with award letters & completion certs.
  • Recommendation letters from 3 reputable client organizations.
