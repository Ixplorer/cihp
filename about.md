# About CIHP — Centre for Integrated Health Programs
> **Institutional Profile & Legacy** | *An African Institution Built on Accountable Delivery*

---

## 🏛️ Executive Summary

**Centre for Integrated Health Programs (CIHP)** is a premier indigenous non-governmental organization promoting equitable, evidence-based public health solutions across **20 Nigerian states**, with a strategic ambition to establish physical footholds across **5 African nations**.

CIHP transitioned in 2010 from ICAP at Columbia University to become a fully registered indigenous Nigerian non-profit institution dedicated to sustainable health systems, clinical excellence, and high-impact partnership delivery.

## 📜 History & Operational Evolution

```
2006 – 2010: Operational Foundations (ICAP at Columbia University)
├── Built clinical infrastructure & trained healthcare workers under PEPFAR funded HIV/AIDS program.
│
2010: Indigenous Transition
├── Direct transition from ICAP to autonomous Nigerian NGO under Dr. Bolanle Oyeledun.
│
2010 – 2022: Multi-State Scale & Grant Stewardship
├── Expanded to 20 states across HIV/AIDS, RMNCH, TB, Laboratory networks, & Digital Health.
├── Administered grants from PEPFAR (CDC), EU, GAVI, Global Fund, and Gates Foundation.
│
2025 – 2026 & Beyond: Global Consortia & Pan-African Expansion
└── Launched REACHOUT (EU/EDCTP3), IMPAACT4HIV (Unitaid), AEARI, & 4th Gates Foundation Award.
└── Advancing 3-year plan to establish 5 Pan-African operational footholds.
```

## 👤 Executive Leadership

### Dr. Bolanle Oyeledun
**Chief Executive Officer & Member, Board of Directors**

Dr. Bolanle Oyeledun has served as Chief Executive Officer of CIHP since 2013, having previously led ICAP Nigeria from 2006 to 2011. Under her leadership, CIHP expanded from a donor transition initiative into an indigenous health powerhouse reaching over 11 million lives across 20 Nigerian states.

* **Education:** MBBS (*University of Ibadan, 1987*), MPH (*University of Lagos, 1995*)
* **Focus Areas:** Public Health Systems, HIV/AIDS Programmatic Scaling, Maternal Health
* **Governance Role:** Executive Oversight & Pan-African Strategy Implementation
