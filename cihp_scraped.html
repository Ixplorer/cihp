Our Collective efforts keep people healthy, alive, and happy. We remain resilient and innovative even as we continue to ensure that we meet the health needs of every Nigerian woman, man, and child.

SDGs

United Nations Sustainable Development Goals

Our mandate aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and is fueled by a passion to ensure no woman, child or man in Nigeria is left behind as we strive to achieve good health and wellbeing for all.

OUR FOOT PRINT

Reached over 11 million lives with life saving health interventions.

We deliver equitable, accountable, evidence based, people centered public health interventions that are transparent and accessible to everyone, regardless of age, sex or gender. We have achieved demonstrable successes and established innovative landmarks over the course of our fifteen (15) years of operations, including experience in rapid programmatic design, mobilization of technical assistance and direct health service delivery.
