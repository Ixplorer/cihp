CIHP Leads European Union-Funded REACHOUT Consortium Tackling Zero-Dose Immunization Gaps

The Centre for Integrated Health Programs (CIHP) officially rollouts the REACHOUT Project—a flagship four-year implementation research partnership funded by the European Union (Global Health EDCTP3). Working across Nigeria, Kenya, and Rwanda alongside 6 European and African research institutions, REACHOUT is translating science into precision health solutions for underserved communities.
